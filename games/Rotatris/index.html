Close a ring

Pieces fall toward the core. Fill every cell of a square ring and that ring clears. Blocks farther out slide inward.

Turn the piece, or the board

Z X rotate the piece. In Manual, A S turn the whole board. Gravity tips toward the heavy side. Clockwork turns after every piece. C holds a piece. ↑ drops it. On a phone, use the round buttons, or swipe.

Stay inside the frame

The brass square is the limit. A block that locks outside it ends the run. Rings build combos, finish contracts, and speed the fall. Your best score is kept on this device.
