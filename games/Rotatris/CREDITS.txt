Rotatris — credits and licenses
================================

This game is an original work. It does not use characters, logos, names, or
music from other games or brands.

Artwork
-------
The enamel tiles, stone board, brass frame, observatory background, icons,
and interface are drawn for Rotatris. No third-party images are included.

Music
-----
"Orrery" is an original musical loop composed for Rotatris and saved as
assets/music/orrery.ogg. It is not copied from a sample library or another
game.

Sound effects
-------------
Short interface and impact sounds are from Kenney's packs:

- Interface Sounds 1.0 (Kenney, 2020)
- Impact Sounds 1.0 (Kenney, 2019)

Both packs are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal).

---------------
